  • Element (I): belief in external structure.
  • Element (II): reference systems (TPS).
  • Element (III): accessible observables.
  • Approach reproduced.

  • ($\alpha$)restrict the accessible observables to the relational algebras themselves;
  • ($\beta$)all operators that commute with the generators of the largest symmetry of the relational algebras are accessible.
  • ($\gamma$)only the gauge-invariant degrees of freedom, those that commute with the constraint of element (I) are accessible.

  • $\simeq$ Indistinguishable at the relational level. $^{\dagger}$ only in a definite $\hat{P}$ ($\hat{x}_{\mathrm{CM}}$) sector.
  • Same approach in a different basis (reparametrization of the relational subsystem).
